Frances Mouttet

My name is Frances Mouttet, and I am a child of art. It has never been a choice for me; painting and creating art are instinctive. It's something I return to to understand myself and the world around me. Painting is my primary medium, but I am open to experimentation and exploration. Every medium offers a different language, and I am always curious to see where and how far I can push my own expression.

My inspiration for all my work is rooted in my environment, especially in the people closest to me. Conversations with my sisters, my parents, and my friends become the starting point for my work. These deep exchanges shape the narratives I choose to explore. As a result, my work centers on human connection. I am particularly drawn to faces; I see them as storytellers, memory, and identity all at once.

Each piece of work carries a part of me, my thoughts, my experiences, my questions, which is why I dread the times I have to part with it. My art is not just something I make; it's my way of surviving the world we live in today. It is something I hold onto that reflects who I am at a given moment in time.

Currently, I am in my third year of university, studying advertising at the Academy of Art University. This field has expanded the way I think about visual communication and storytelling. Advertising has taught me how to communicate emotion and storytelling into intentional, impactful visuals. It has pushed me to consider audience, message, and meaning in new ways. Advertising has allowed my creative practice to grow beyond personal expression into something that can connect with others.

At its core, my work is an ongoing conversation between who I am and who I am becoming.
