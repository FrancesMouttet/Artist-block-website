Reading

Reading lets me step into someone else's world for a while. Because as I read, I visualize, my brain can create new ideas without the pressure of thinking them up myself. It fills my mind with emotions and many different perspectives. I'm not creative, but I am absorbent, and eventually, something from what I read finds its way back into my work.
