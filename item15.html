Looking at other people's work

I tend to spend a lot of time looking at what other artists have created, and sometimes I get caught up comparing, but it can also be extremely inspiring. I switch my brain and try to observe instead of compare. I ask myself questions: What do I like? What makes me feel something? How would I have done differently? It inspires me that creativity exists everywhere, and that these artists have felt the exact feeling I am feeling and have gotten out and created masterpieces. This step always makes me feel hope.
