Boxing

Boxing is a new step in my process. I have always boxed, but recently it has become one of the most important tools in this process. It gives my frustration some relief. I can let it out physically. Every punch and kick feels like releasing the pressure that was blocking me. By the time the session is over, I always feel like I can take on the world, my mind feels free, and I have made space for something new.
