Going on a walk

Going on a walk feels like pressing a quiet reset button. I step outside, feel the sun, and suddenly everything becomes bigger than whatever was stuck in my head—the sky, the air, the small sounds I usually ignore. I don’t try to force ideas; I just notice what’s around me. The way trees stand, how the grass grows, the tire tracks on the ground, the distant sound of people hitting golf balls. It reminds me that the world is always moving and full of inspiration, and that I often forget to look because I’m too stuck in my own head. After my walks, I feel lighter, more centered, and open to creating again.
