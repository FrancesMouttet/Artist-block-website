Complaining

Sometimes I just need to say it out loud. I will sit one of my sisters down and word-vomit everything. Complaining doesn't solve it, but it releases the pressure of holding it all in. I get to hear myself and make the problem feel smaller. I can hear how silly I sound sometimes, and this steo makes me feel lighter. When I complain, I reflect, which helps me get through unnecessary self-deprecating thoughts.
