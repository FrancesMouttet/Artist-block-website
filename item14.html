Painting hoodie

A lot of my home clothes are covered in paint specs, but there is this one particular hoodie that is sacred to creating. It feels like proof that I am an artist when I wear it. This hoodie has sat through hours of work with me. It's been part of my most beloved work. I really believe it holds my artist energy. It makes me feel like a true artist when I put it on, even before I start.
