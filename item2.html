Have a crash out

Sometimes the best thing I can do is allow myself to have a complete mental breakdown. I let myself become the most dramatic version of me. I become angry and frustrated, and I scream. I can even go as far as flinging my limbs around. I stop pretending I'm fine and just feel everything. Then, after all that chaos, my brain settles, like a storm clearing out. The pressure loosens, and I remind myself that creating isn't supposed to feel like suffocating. That it's okay to break down if it means I can build something real again.
