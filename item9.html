Staring at a fresh canvas

This is the step I hate the most. I set up my station, I lay out everything I need to start a new piece of work, and I just start. A blank canvas feels intimidating, like it's judging me before I have even started. I will stare and stare and stare, and complain internally. Once I get past this feeling, I come to terms with the fact that this black canvas is actually a space of endless possibilities, which is also a daunting thought; however, due to not starting there, there is nothing there for me to hate, nothing is ruined, it's just a space for me to wait in.
