Sketchbook

My sketchbook, the bane of my existence, is full of half-starts, messy lines, doodles, and a lot of ideas I gave up on quickly. I flip through, waiting to see if anything catches my attention. As I flip, I remind myself that there aren't failures; it's proof that I try and will continue to try. There's something very comforting about watching unfinished; I am reminded that not everything needs to be completed to have value. Sometimes this step helps; sometimes it's hopeless. Either way, it keeps my creativity moving rather than captive.
