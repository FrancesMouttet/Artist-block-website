Be a Child

Creating with a child is my first and most important survival tool. It's about letting go of pressure, expectations, the need to be good, and instead creating just to create. In this moment, you get to see the world from a child's eye; there is no overthinking, no comparison, just play. Watching my nephew create art reminds me that creativity isn't meant to be perfect or strategic; it's just free. When I return to that mindset. I stop trying to control the outcome and start trusting the process again. It reminds me that I create, not for validation but for the pure joy of it.
