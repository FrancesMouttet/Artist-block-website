Starting something, quitting, and starting again

I let myself start thinking without committing to finishing them. If I get bored or stuck, I leave it and move on. I can come back to it or start again; when I have reached this point in even attempting to create it, it feels like a win. There are no rules that say I have to get it right the first time. So starting over and over again is just a part of figuring out where I want to go.
