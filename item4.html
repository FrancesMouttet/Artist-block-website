Pinterest

Scrolling through my Pinterest feels like tiny sparks are hitting my brain. I can get confused by this app; I get to see colors, textures, moods, and random images, and my mind starts to spin. Each post I pin in my inspiration blog feels like a step closer. It's not a forceful exercise; it's as easy as saving an image because you like how that particular color looks. The more I scroll, the more I find myself in some sort of pattern; inspiration is really being built without much effort. It feels like letting my brain relax instead of demanding answers.
