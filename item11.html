Dance break

Few songs on my curated playlist make me want to dance, but when they appear in the rotation, my body feels this pull to get up and dance—something I just need to move—no plan, just skating and jumping everything out. I sing, I dance, and then I can feel the tension drip off of me. It restores my energy, and it leaves me feeling happy. These dance dreams always make me feel more alive.
