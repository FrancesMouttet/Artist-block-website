Notes

My notes app, an app I keep locked on my phone. If anyone were to read through it, I know they would think something is wrong with me. It's full of half-sentences, random words, and ideas that don't connect. I don't judge anything I write or have written on my phone because this step often saves me. It's when I read back, and one small word can grow into a full concept.
