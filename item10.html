Rewatching old work

Where I paint, I am surrounded by my past pieces. Looking back at my old work reminds me that I am capable and that I have not lost it. It's looking at a past version of myself and reminding me that I can get back there; I just need to be patient. Many of the pieces I am surrounded by have survived the artist block, so it's a great reminder.
