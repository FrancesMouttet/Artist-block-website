Sitting in complete silence

I will sit in complete silence, and at first, it feels very scary to be left alone with just your thoughts, no distractions, no noise to hide behind. But I found that if I sit for long enough, my thoughts start to settle instead of racing. Ideas can come quietly, like a whisper, waiting for me to reach out and grab them.
